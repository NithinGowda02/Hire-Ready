Help Center

Quick answers for common CareerAI questions.

How do I reset my password?

Open the login page and click Forgot your password?. If the email exists in the system, CareerAI will send reset instructions.

How do I improve my resume results?

Complete your profile with strong experience, projects, education, and skills before generating a resume.

Where can I find jobs?

After signing in, use the Jobs page to browse listings and the dashboard to keep track of your activity.

Need more support?

Share the issue details with your project maintainer or support contact, including what you clicked and what happened.
